Clawd Chain · public mint

Mint without the creator key

The creator opens the collection. Any other wallet can mint until the supply cap is met or the sale is closed. The asset keeps the collection name, and the minter becomes the owner.

Open this page over HTTP

A file: URL cannot load the mint rules in every browser. From the repo root run python3 -m http.server 8787 and open http://127.0.0.1:8787/mint/.

The creator key is not required. Enter a minter and mint.
