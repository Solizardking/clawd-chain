What is Alpenglow?

Alpenglow is a consensus protocol for Solana, proposed by Anza researchers and specified in SIMD-0326. It retires TowerBFT and Proof-of-History from the core consensus path and introduces two components:

  • Votor: voting and finalization. Per Anza, a block is finalized in a single round of voting if 80% of stake participates, or in two rounds if only 60% of stake is responsive.
  • Rotor: block propagation. It refines Turbine's erasure-coded approach, uses node bandwidth in proportion to stake, and relays through a single layer of nodes instead of Turbine's multi-layer tree.

The design aims for much faster, deterministic finality than TowerBFT. See Anza's announcement and the SIMD for details. We don't publish benchmark numbers of our own. Use the live counters above.
